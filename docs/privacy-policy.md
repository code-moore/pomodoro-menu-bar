# Privacy Policy (draft)

*Source for the Mac app's privacy policy, published at
https://quietpomodoro.com/mac/privacy/. Keep the two in step.*

**Last updated:** 18 October 2026

Pomodoro is a menu bar app for planning your day in focus blocks, made by
CODE MOORE LTD. It is built to work without collecting anything about you.

## What we collect

Nothing. Pomodoro has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no
tracking.

## What stays on your Mac

- **Your tasks and settings** are saved on your Mac, in the app's preferences
  file (`~/Library/Preferences/com.codemoore.pomodoro.menubar.plist` for the
  version downloaded from our website). Deleting the app leaves this file in
  place; delete it too if you want them gone. The Mac App Store version keeps
  them in its own container, which macOS removes with the app.
- **Your calendar**, if you allow access: Pomodoro reads today's events from
  the calendars on your Mac so it can schedule your tasks around them. Events
  are read on your Mac, used only to build your day's plan, and are not
  stored by the app or sent anywhere. You can turn individual calendars off in
  Settings, and withdraw access at any time in System Settings > Privacy &
  Security > Calendars.

## Update checks (website version only)

The version downloaded from quietpomodoro.com checks for updates about once a
day, and whenever you choose Check for Updates, by fetching
`https://quietpomodoro.com/downloads/mac/appcast.xml`. Updates download from
the same site. Like any web request, these reach our host, Cloudflare, with
your IP address and a user agent that includes the app and macOS versions.
Nothing else is sent. The Mac App Store version is updated by the App Store
and makes no requests of its own.

## Links

The phone icon in the panel opens quietpomodoro.com in your browser, where
the website's own privacy policy applies.

## Children

Pomodoro does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including
children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new
date.
